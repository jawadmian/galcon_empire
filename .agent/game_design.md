 A deep, systemic "Galactic Ant-Farm" where the player acts as an incorporeal observer and shaper, watching centuries of history unfold in minutes through the interactions of ambitious leaders and sprawling star-empires.

Core Gameplay Loop:

Observe: Watch planets, fleets, and leaders interact. The galaxy is alive with events, trade, diplomacy, and war.

Influence: Use influence to subtly guide events. You can't directly control ships or planets, but you can sway leaders, fund factions, or trigger crises.

Evolve: Empires rise and fall. New technologies emerge, ideologies clash, and the map changes over time. Your goal is to shape the galaxy's destiny.
